    🎃 About Me

    I am now a Ph.D. Candidate in School of Computer Science, Nanjing University, supervised by Prof. Haipeng Dai and Prof. Wei Wang. I received my B.S. degree from Dalian University of Technology in 2022. My research interests include ML for Signal Processing, LLM for Wireless Sensing, Ubiquitous Computing and Mobile Computing.
